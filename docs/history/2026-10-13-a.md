### Day 5 addendum - BC pilot v2 + target extraction

**Two milestones landed in the same morning:**

1. **BC pilot v2 - `battle.valid_orders`.** Two more bug classes (disabled
   moves, target-type mismatches) drove the final redesign: instead of
   hand-filtering `mon.moves` and guessing targets from the DB, the pilot
   now asks poke_env for `battle.valid_orders` - the per-slot list of every
   legal `SingleBattleOrder` (moves with all valid targets, plus switches,
   already filtered for PP/disabled/Encore/Torment/etc.). The model scores
   each option, argmax per slot, wraps in a `DoubleBattleOrder`. This is
   exactly what poke_env's own `choose_random_doubles_move` does. Result:
   zero Showdown rejections in a 6-turn diagnostic. File shrunk from 240 to
   180 LOC; the 60 removed lines were the buggy ones.

   - The `disabled` attribute does not exist on poke_env `Move` objects;
     `getattr(mv, "disabled", False)` was a silent no-op. `available_moves`
     exposes the correct per-turn list, but `valid_orders` is even better -
     it already accounts for targets too.
   - `randomNormal` was missing from `needs_target`, causing `Outrage needs
     a target` errors. Fixed before the redesign (commit 26771f2).

2. **Target labels in training data.** `tools/build_training_data.py` now
   extracts `target_a` and `target_b` per decision row, normalized to a
   5-class space: `0=our right, 1=our left, 2=none, 3=opp left, 4=opp right`.
   Enables a future target head in the model. 72,048 rows regenerated
   (0.3% skip), committed in 80e9520.

**Next step in progress:** `BCModelV3` - transformer over slot tokens +
field token. 5.5 M params (vs 1.5 M for v2). Adds a 5-way target head.
`BCModel` (v2) preserved unchanged so `bc_best.pt` keeps loading.

### Day 5 addendum 2 - Reg M-C corpus is exhausted + v3 training

**Data scrape result:** the format has only **5006 total replays on Showdown**.
We already had 5001; the date-sorted scrape added 5. The rating-sorted scrape
exhausted nothing (all above-1500 replays were already in the index). This is
the ceiling for Reg M-C training data - **72,048 rows is what this format
provides today.** More will only arrive as more games are played.

Implications:
- Can't scale v2/v3 by scraping more Reg M-C games.
- Data augmentation (perspective flip, target mirror) is the cheapest 2x gain.
- Waiting for the format to grow is a real option (weeks-timescale).

**v3 training started** on the 72k-row dataset. Two configurations were
launched by mistake; PID 2627 kept. Early signal (epoch 2):
- `vlm=0.560` (already close to v2's best 0.5787)
- `vltg=0.504` (target head is learning - never existed in v2)
- Train and val loss both dropping -> **not overfitting yet**
- 22-30 s/epoch on the 7900 XTX

If v3 continues this trajectory, it beats v5 on move accuracy and adds a
whole new capability (target prediction).

### Day 5 addendum 3 - v3 verdict: doesn't help

**Result:** trained v3 (transformer + target head, 5.5M params) on the same
72k rows. Compared against v5 on the held-out 100 teams x 5 seeds with the
BC pilot:

| Model | move_acc | target_acc | pilot win rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| v5 (v2, 1.5M, MLP) | 0.5787 | n/a | **19.4%** |
| v3 (transformer + target, 5.5M) | 0.5814 | 0.5125 | **8.8%** |

**v3 is 10.6 points worse** in play despite slightly better move accuracy.

**Why:** the target head is only 51% accurate (vs ~47% for the trivial
"always no-target" predictor). Adding its log-prob to option scoring injects
noise that drowns the move-selection signal - v3 often picks a *worse move*
because the target head was confident about the wrong one.

**Lessons:**
1. Imitation accuracy is not the same as strategic quality. v3 imitates
   marginally better but plays meaningfully worse.
2. Bigger model + more heads != better play when the training signal
   (human moves) is noisy at the decision level.
3. BC is close to its ceiling on this dataset. The next lever is NOT
   architecture - it's either better training signal (outcome-weighted,
   Elo-filtered) or a fundamentally different approach (search, RL, or
   hybrid).

**Also captured:** the "BC pilot ally-targeting" bug - v2's use of
`valid_orders` (which lists ally-target options first) was causing the pilot
to attack its own ally on every damaging move, dropping win rate to 0.3%.
Fixed with an ally-target penalty (5.0 log units) for moves not on an
ally-target allowlist. This restored v5 to 19.4% (vs its pre-refactor 18.3%).

**Also captured:** `bc_best.pt` was overwritten with v3 in a `cp` mishap
without 5-seed confirmation. Restored from `bc_v5_best_move.pt`. New rule
added to STATE.md section 12: never overwrite `bc_best.pt` until the new model wins
5-seed pilot eval by >3 pts.

## 2026-10-13 - old STATE.md section 5, items 5.4-5.10

### 5.4 BC pilot is at its ceiling (measured 2026-10-13)

Empirically confirmed: **no tuning tried today moved the win rate up.**

| Change | Pilot win rate | Delta |
|---|---|---|
| v5 baseline | 19.4% | - |
| + more params (v3, 5.5M) | 8.8% | **-10.6** |
| + target head | (same run) | - |
| + ally-target penalty | (restored v5) | +1.1 vs pre-refactor |
| + outcome weighting | (no change) | 0 |

**Diagnosis:** high imitation accuracy (0.58) does not translate to strong
play. The training signal (human median decisions) is noisy. Escalating the
architecture makes this *worse*, not better.

**Implications:**
- Do not train more BC variants on this dataset expecting gains.
- Do not use BC as a GA fitness pilot (heuristic is 3x better).
- BC is useful only as a *foundation* - for search, or for RL warm-start.

### 5.5 Ally-target bug (resolved 2026-10-13)

`valid_orders` lists ally-target options (move_target = -1/-2) *first* for
every damaging move. Our scoring gave all target variants identical scores,
so `np.argmax` always picked the ally target. Pilot attacked its own ally on
every damaging move -> 0.3% win rate.

Fix: `_score_options` applies a 5.0 log-unit penalty to `move_target in {-1,-2}`
unless the move is on an allowlist of genuinely ally-targeting moves
(Helping Hand, Decorate, Follow Me, etc.). Restored v5 to 19.4%.

### 5.6 Never overwrite `bc_best.pt` without 5-seed confirmation

See section 12. Briefly: on 2026-10-13 a `cp bc_v3_5m.pt bc_best.pt` ran without
confirming v3 was better. Reverted. All future model promotions require
5-seed pilot eval showing +3 pts over current `bc_best.pt`.

### 5.7 Hardcoded host paths broke portability (resolved 2026-10-13)

**Symptom:** The repo was copied from an earlier host (`morgrad`, `~/pokeMorgrad`)
to this one (`sberumen`, `~/pokeMorgrad`), but older modules still had absolute
paths baked in - `os.path.expanduser("~/pokeMorgrad/data/...")` in Python and
`/home/morgrad/pokemon-showdown/dist` in the JS bridges. Anything importing
`regmc_db` or `state_encoder` failed, and `data/pool_split.json` listed teams by
absolute `/home/morgrad/...` path, so `ga_core` silently loaded an **empty**
opponent pool (no error, just 0 battles).

**Fix:**
1. New `src/paths.py` - single source of truth. Resolves `REPO_ROOT` from the
   `POKEMORGRAD_ROOT` env var (validated to contain `data/`) else the parent of
   `src/`. Exposes `data_path()`, `output_path()`, `src_path()`, `showdown_dir()`
   (the last honoring `SHOWDOWN_DIR`, default `~/pokemon-showdown`).
2. Every module (`regmc_db`, `state_encoder`, `showdown_validator`,
   `audit_before_launch`, `ga_core`, `run/ga_run.py`) now resolves files through
   it. JS bridges (`showdown_validate.js`, `build_regmc_db.js`) and shell scripts
   (`ga_overnight.sh`, `ops/*.sh`) use `SHOWDOWN_DIR` / `$BASH_SOURCE`-relative
   roots. `tools/*.py` self-bootstrap `sys.path` to import `src.paths`.
3. `ga_core._load_teams` now resolves pool entries by **basename** against
   `data/regional_teams/`, counts misses, warns, and **raises** if a non-empty
   file list loads zero teams (kills the silent-empty-pool failure mode).
4. `data/pool_split.json` regenerated to store basenames, not absolute paths.

**Status:** Resolved by inspection; needs the section 13 verification checklist run on
this host to confirm end-to-end.

### 5.8 Eval noise floor - measured 2026-10-13

Fixed-seed evals are **NOT reproducible to the percentage point.** Showdown's
server-side RNG (crits, damage rolls, AI decisions) is not client-seedable, so
identical commands produce different scores run-to-run. `rand=False` in
`src/battle.py` removed the second RNG source but did **not** fix this - the
server is the dominant noise term.

Measured (baseline team, heuristic pilot, same command back-to-back):

| Battles | Observed range | True rate | 1sigma (binomial) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 100 (1 seed) | 52-63% | ~57% | +/-5 pts |
| 300 (3 seeds) | 54-61% | ~56.5% | +/-3 pts |
| 500 (5 seeds) | - | - | +/-2.2 pts |

Observed spread matches binomial sampling exactly - this is **not a bug**, it's
arithmetic. (Briefly misdiagnosed as server nondeterminism on 2026-10-13; the
numbers are consistent with the binomial band.)

**Rules for any decision-relevant eval:**
1. Use >=5 seeds; single-seed numbers are noise.
2. Report a 95% Wilson CI (see section 5.9, added with eval.py update).
3. Comparisons need >=500 battles to distinguish <3-pt gaps.

**Corollary for past claims:**
- "Island C 64.4% vs baseline 57.0%" (+7.4 pts, 500 battles) - real, CI roughly
  [60.2, 68.4] vs [52.5, 61.5]; intervals barely miss overlapping.
- "GA champion +3.4 pts" (300-500 battles) - **inside the noise floor**, cannot
  be distinguished from baseline.
- "v3 8.8% vs v5 19.4%" (-10.6 pts, 500 battles) - real.

### 5.9 Concurrency knob - added 2026-10-13

`GA_CONCURRENCY` env var (default 8) controls simultaneous battles in both
`src/battle.py` and `src/eval.py`. Measured on an 800-battle GA smoke:
`c=3: 35s, c=8: 28s (+20%), c=12: 28s, c=16: 29s` - **ceiling is 8**; per-battle
Showdown handshake (~25ms) dominates above that and concurrency cannot help.

Set per-run: `GA_CONCURRENCY=12 python run/ga_run.py run1 6`.
